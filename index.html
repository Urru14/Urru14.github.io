About

PhD researcher in Chemical Engineering combining density functional theory, scientific Python and HPC with experimental materials characterisation to study sodium-ion conducting solid electrolytes for batteries. I build reproducible DFT and data-analysis workflows, run calculations on Linux clusters from the terminal, and am building a training dataset of electrolyte materials for machine learning. Background in quantum simulation (Qiskit, Fortran) from IIT Roorkee; gold-medallist MSc Physics.
